"Most businesses don't need more theory. They need someone to show them the next step."

Rana Nazar Muhammad

He studied marketing in Lahore and spent years running campaigns for brands and small businesses. Again and again he saw good businesses struggle because nobody had explained the basics in plain words, so he started writing them down. Those notes became his first book. Today his books are read in Pakistan and abroad. He also runs live lectures on Zoom so readers can ask him questions directly.

Policies

Terms, privacy and refunds.

Terms

Lectures are for the person who registered. Please don't share Zoom links or recordings with others. Be respectful in live sessions; anyone disrupting a class may be removed. Lecture dates can change, and registered students are told by email and WhatsApp.

Privacy

We collect your name, email, WhatsApp number and payment details only to register you and send your lecture links. We don't sell or share your information. You can ask us to delete it at any time by sending a message.

Refunds

If you can't attend a paid live lecture, tell us at least 24 hours before it starts for a full refund or a free seat in a later lecture. Recorded lectures can't be refunded once the link is sent. Book refunds are handled by Amazon, Etsy and Gumroad.
